About Luxury Medical Group

Luxury Medical Group was founded in Denver, Colorado with a single belief: that high-level medical care and a truly concierge experience should exist in the same space. Our focus is on GLP-1–based weight management and advanced peptide therapies, delivered with the kind of attention to detail you’d expect from a luxury service.

Rather than high-volume, rushed visits, we intentionally keep our client panel small. That allows us to spend more time listening, planning, and following up—so you feel supported from the first consultation through every step of your wellness journey.

Luxury Medical Group is built on the belief that elite care isn't about shortcuts - it's about intention, expertise, and outcomes.

Denver Roots, Concierge Reach

Luxury Medical Group is based in Denver, Colorado, serving clients across the metro area and beyond. While our permanent physical clinic address is still being finalized, we currently provide care by appointment through a concierge model that balances in-person visits (where available) with secure virtual care.

As we grow, we remain committed to a simple promise: to combine evidence-based medicine with the kind of thoughtful, one-to-one attention that makes you feel like more than a patient—you feel like a priority.
