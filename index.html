FAQ

Answers to common questions.

Do you have a physical clinic location?

Luxury Medical Group is headquartered in Denver, Colorado. Our permanent clinic space is currently being finalized. In the meantime, we serve patients across Colorado through secure telehealth visits and select in-person touchpoints as available.

Are GLP-1 or peptide therapies guaranteed?

No. GLP-1 medications and peptide therapies are only prescribed when medically appropriate after a full clinical evaluation. Some individuals will not be candidates, and results vary. All medications carry risks and potential side effects, which will be reviewed with you before any decision is made.

Do you work with other providers?

Yes. With your permission, we can coordinate care and share relevant information with your primary care clinician, specialist, or therapist to help keep your care aligned and cohesive.

Do you accept insurance?

At this time, Luxury Medical Group operates as a cash-pay concierge practice. We can provide documentation you may submit to your insurance provider or HSA/FSA where applicable, but reimbursement is not guaranteed.
