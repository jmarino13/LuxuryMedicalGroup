Frequently Asked Questions

Do you have a physical clinic location?

Luxury Medical Group is based in Denver, Colorado. We are currently offering concierge services by appointment while we finalize our permanent physical clinic location.

Who is a candidate for GLP-1 therapy?

Candidacy is determined by a licensed medical provider after a full review of your medical history, current medications, goals, and lab work where appropriate. Not everyone will qualify for GLP-1 treatment.

Are peptide therapies safe?

All therapies carry potential risks and benefits. Our team will review these with you in detail and only recommend therapies that are clinically appropriate based on your individual profile.

Do you accept insurance?

At this time, Luxury Medical Group operates as a concierge clinic and does not bill insurance directly. We can provide visit summaries you may share with your insurer, but reimbursement is not guaranteed.
